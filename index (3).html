How Stampee works

No complicated contracts. No full wraps. Just stickers, taxis, and your brand all over the city.

01 🎯

Pick your campaign

Decide on your Stampee sticker design and choose how many taxis will display your brand. Sign up to our simple online agreement.

02 🚕

We match you with drivers

We connect your campaign with verified, active taxi drivers in your chosen area. We handle all driver communication.

03 🏷️

Sticker goes on

High-quality, weatherproof vinyl stickers are applied professionally. Car-wash safe. Removable cleanly in minutes.

04 📍

Your brand hits the streets

Watch your brand move across the city. We send you a monthly coverage report with mileage data and a master report at the end of the campaign.
