How Stampee works

No complicated contracts. No full wraps. Just stickers, taxis, and your brand all over the city.

01 🎯

Pick your campaign

Choose your sticker placement, the city you want to target, and how many taxis. Sign a simple monthly agreement online.

02 🚕

We match you with drivers

We connect your campaign with verified, active taxi drivers in your chosen area. We handle all driver communication.

03 🏷️

Sticker goes on

High-quality, weatherproof vinyl stickers are applied by the driver. Car-wash safe. Removable cleanly in minutes.

04 📍

Your brand hits the streets

Watch your brand move across the city. We send you a monthly coverage report with mileage data.
